Dojo Code & Etiquette

All students are expected to follow our traditional code of respect and discipline.

SAMURAI Dojo Rules
  1. Respect: Bow upon entering and leaving the Dojo hall.
  2. Punctuality: Arrive 10 minutes prior to training start time.
  3. Uniform: Clean traditional Gi (or sports wear for trial) is mandatory.
  4. Safety: Follow coach instructions instantly and keep fingernails trimmed.
