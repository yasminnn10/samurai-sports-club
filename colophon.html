Dojo Rules & Etiquette

We follow strict traditional martial arts principles during every training session.

SAMURAI Dojo Code of Conduct
  1. Respect: Bow when entering and leaving the Dojo.
  2. Punctuality: Arrive 10 minutes before the scheduled class starts.
  3. Uniform: Wear a clean traditional Dogi (karate suit) or sport kit.
  4. Discipline: Listen attentively to Sensei instructions at all times.
